Noel Notes

A private Christmas gift list and budget tracker for iPhone

Know who you're buying for, what you've spent, and what's left to wrap, in one calm list your kids can't peek at. Noel Notes is a Christmas gift planner: add everyone on your list, give each person a budget, and follow every gift from idea to wrapped.

Download on the App Store

  • Free, with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads
  • No account and no tracking. Your list stays on your devices and in your own iCloud
  • Optional Face ID, Touch ID or passcode lock
  • For iPhone, iOS 18 or later. In US dollars, Canadian dollars, British pounds or Australian dollars

What it does

Everyone on your list

  • Add family, friends, coworkers and neighbors, sorted into groups.
  • Give each person a budget and see what you've spent on them.
  • Keep sizes, favorite colors and wish-list hints in their notes.

Every gift, from idea to wrapped

  • Swipe a gift from Idea to Planned, Ordered, Received, Wrapped and Done.
  • Save a photo, the store, a link, the estimate and the price you paid.
  • Paste a store link to fill in the product name, price and picture when the store page provides them.
  • Deleted something by mistake? Tap Undo.

A Christmas budget that adds up

  • Set one holiday budget and see what's spent, what's set aside for each person, and what's left.
  • Get a gentle warning when the gifts you've planned for someone go past their budget.
  • Charts by gift status and by group.

Wrapping night, sorted

  • A To Wrap checklist shows every gift that has arrived, grouped by person.
  • Gifts still on their way are listed below it.

Keeps the surprise a surprise

  • Lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. The lock covers all your devices.
  • The list is hidden in the app switcher and while the screen is shared or recorded.
  • The Dashboard shows prices, not gift names, so a quick glance spoils nothing.
  • Share a wish list with family that leaves out prices and anything already bought.
  • Store links open inside the app, so they don't end up in Safari's history.
  • Exported files have plain names, like Notes_2026.pdf.

Made for the season

  • Optional reminders 30, 14, 7, 3 and 1 day before Christmas.
  • Gentle snowfall, which you can turn off.
  • Export your list as a spreadsheet (CSV) or a PDF.
  • Dark Mode, larger text and VoiceOver support.

Questions

  • Can my kids or family see my gift list?

    Not if you turn on the lock. Noel Notes asks for Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode when it opens, hides the list in the app switcher and during screen sharing, and never shows gift names on the Dashboard.

  • Is Noel Notes free?

    Yes. There's no subscription, no in-app purchase and no ads.

  • Do I need an account?

    No. There's nothing to sign up for. Everything works right after you install it.

  • Does it sync between my iPhone and my partner's iPhone?

    It syncs between devices signed in to the same Apple Account, through your own private iCloud. It doesn't share a list between two different Apple Accounts.

  • Can I track my Christmas spending per person?

    Yes. Each person has their own budget, and the Dashboard shows your whole Christmas budget: spent, set aside and left over.

  • Is it for birthdays and other occasions too?

    No. Noel Notes is made just for Christmas, so everything in it, from the reminders to the wrapping checklist, is about the holidays.

  • Is there an Android version?

    No. Noel Notes is for iPhone only.

  • What data does it collect?

    None. Read the Privacy Policy.
