Noel Notes

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

The short version

  • The app doesn't collect, store, sell or share any of your information.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics tools, no tracking.
  • Your gift list stays on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your private iCloud account. We can't see it.

In this policy, "we" means the developer of Noel Notes, and "your list" means everything you add to the app.

  • What we collect

    Nothing. Noel Notes has no servers and no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. Nothing from your list or your device is sent to us.

  • Where your list is stored

    On your device and, if iCloud is on for Noel Notes, in your private iCloud account, protected by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it.

  • The app lock

    Face ID, Touch ID and your passcode are checked by iOS; Noel Notes only learns whether the check succeeded. The lock's on/off setting is stored on your device and in your private iCloud account, so it applies to all your devices.

  • Photos and the camera

    Noel Notes only receives the photos you pick or take; it can't see the rest of your photo library. Photos taken in the app aren't saved to your library. Your photos are stored as part of your list.

  • Adding a gift from a web link, and checking its price

    You can paste a link to a product page to add it as a gift ("Add from Link"), or ask the app to check a saved gift's current price ("Check Price"). For both, your device visits that product page directly, like a browser, to read the product's name, price and photo. The website sees a normal visit; nothing from your list is sent to it or to us. Common tracking codes are removed from saved links.

  • Opening gift links

    Links open in Apple's in-app browser, so they stay out of your Safari history. Noel Notes can't see what you view or type there.

  • Reminders

    Reminders are scheduled on your device and contain nothing from your list.

  • Sharing and exporting

    Nothing leaves the app unless you share it. A shared wishlist includes only the person's name and the names and links of gifts marked Idea or Planned, never prices or gifts you've bought. Exported CSV and PDF files go only where you send them.

  • Crash reports

    If you've allowed sharing analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. They don't include your list or identify you.

  • Children

    We don't collect information from anyone, including children.

  • Deleting your data

    We hold none of your data, so there's nothing to request from us. To remove your list, delete the app; to remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Noel Notes.

  • Changes

    If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.

  • Contact

    Email klaudzstudio@gmail.com.
